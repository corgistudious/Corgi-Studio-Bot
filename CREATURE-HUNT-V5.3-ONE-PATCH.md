# Creature Hunt V5.3 — One Patch

- Keeps existing CreatureProfile progression/data intact.
- DeveloperSettings now controls Creature V5 stars, recovery, PvE/Boss rewards, missions and CXu rewards.
- CXu is the shared/global economy reward for Adventure, Boss, Daily/Weekly missions and Account Level-Up.
- Pet Essence and Orbs remain Creature Hunt-specific progression resources.
- Default CXu balance: Adventure 20 + stage×2; Boss 80 + stage×8; Daily 250; Weekly 1000; Account Level-Up 100 + level×25.
- All CXu values can be changed from /dev Creature Hunt V5.
- Creature Hunt command localization remains configured for 12 locales.
- Player data is not reset by the V5 developer defaults reset.
