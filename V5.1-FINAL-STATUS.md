## Localization quality note
New V5.1 Market/Seasonal surfaces contain explicit 12-language sentence translations. Older V5 surfaces are routed through a compatibility lexicon for the ten added languages. This provides locale routing but is not equivalent to native, sentence-by-sentence translation review. Do not claim native-complete localization until those legacy strings are individually translated/reviewed.
