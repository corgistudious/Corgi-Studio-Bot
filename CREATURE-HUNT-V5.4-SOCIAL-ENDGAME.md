# Creature Hunt V5.4 — Social Endgame

Adds Breeding, safe two-party Pet Trading, CXu Pet Marketplace, and Guild Pet War.

- Breeding: two distinct Lv.10+ Pets, 500 CXu, 12h cooldown, offspring starts Lv.1 / ★0; appearance remains catalog-based.
- Trading: recipient must explicitly accept within 10 minutes. Locked, favorite, team, and companion Pets cannot move.
- Marketplace: Pet is escrowed when listed; minimum 100 CXu; 5% sink fee on sale; cancel restores the Pet.
- Guild Pet War: challenge a member of another clan; accepted war measures up to the top 10 clan members' active Pet teams with ±10% battle variance; winner receives 250 Clan Points.
- 12-language runtime strings and command descriptions included for EN, VI, DE, ES, FR, ID, JA, KO, PT-BR, PT-PT, zh-CN, zh-TW.
- Existing Creature progression and player data are preserved.
