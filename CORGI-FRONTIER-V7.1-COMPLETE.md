# Corgi Frontier V7.1 — Complete Long-Term Foundation

Corgi Frontier is the flagship persistent game of Corgi-Bot. It replaces the old multi-game Game Hub direction.

## Core law
- One click = one real action.
- No nested gameplay tabs.
- No seasonal progression reset.
- No weekly-rank race or monthly Game Hub tournament dependency.
- Player history and progression are permanent.

## Long-term phases
1. Origin — learn Explore, Gather, Fish, Camp and first discoveries.
2. Expansion — unlock regions, build the permanent base and master crafting.
3. Naturalist — collect creatures with rarity, trait, potential and variants.
4. Evolution — train bonds, levels and creature evolution.
5. Expedition — timed offline expeditions with escalating risk/reward.
6. Research — artifacts unlock lore and permanent discovery history.
7. World — permanent community contribution layer and Frontier Marks.
8. Deep Frontier — Abyss / Ancient Civilization / Starfall Reaches.
9. Eternal Frontier — Rank 160+ world intended as the first long-term endgame layer.
10. Future Worlds — new regions/content can be appended without wiping profiles.

## Persistent systems
Explorer Rank/XP, region discovery %, Energy, Supplies, distance, resources, rare resources, Fishing Codex, Creature Codex, creature rarity/traits/potential/variants/bond/evolution, artifacts, lore, secrets (hidden total), Base buildings, Masteries, Crafting, timed Expeditions, Achievements, Legacy, World Contributions and Frontier Marks.

## World ladder
Origin Plains (R1) → Whispering Forest (R5) → Frozen Frontier (R12) → Lost Ocean (R22) → Sky Archipelago (R35) → Ember Continent (R50) → The Abyss (R70) → Ancient Civilization (R95) → Starfall Reaches (R125) → Eternal Frontier (R160).

This is a complete *foundation architecture*, not a finite content promise: future worlds, creatures, artifacts and secrets are deliberately append-only so the flagship game can continue for years.
