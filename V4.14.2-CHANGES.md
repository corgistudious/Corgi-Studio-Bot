# Corgi-Bot V4.14.2 — Corgi AI Unhinged Mode

- Increased casual-chat personality from light cheeky banter to bold, highly spontaneous comedy.
- Added stronger sarcasm, playful roasting, irony, deadpan humor, absurd comparisons, clap-backs, and punchline-first replies.
- Added a modern social-chat feel: faster reactions, shorter answers, better slang/energy mirroring, less customer-service wording.
- Keeps Corgi AI original; it borrows only general conversational qualities from modern assistants, not another product's exact persona or catchphrases.
- Increased response variation using randomized chaos directions and higher creativity settings.
- Serious, factual, coding, troubleshooting, and safety-sensitive questions automatically prioritize accuracy and clarity over jokes.
- Added explicit guardrails against hateful insults, threats, cruel humiliation, sensitive-trait attacks, and invented accusations.
- No slash-command changes are required for this release.
