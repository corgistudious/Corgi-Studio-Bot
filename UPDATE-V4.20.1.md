# V4.20.1 — Sic Bo & Roulette Panel UX

- `/taixiu` and `?taixiu` now open an interactive Sic Bo betting table when no quick-bet arguments are supplied.
- `/roullette` and `?roulette` now open an interactive European Roulette table when no quick-bet arguments are supplied.
- Sic Bo panel: Big, Small, Any Triple, Total 4–17, Single/Double/Triple 1–6, and two-face pairs.
- Roulette panel: Red/Black, Odd/Even, halves, dozens, columns, Straight, Split, Street, Corner, Six Line, Zero Trio, and Basket.
- Bet amount is entered through Discord modal UI.
- Tables are owner-locked so other users cannot operate another player's controls.
- Existing slash/prefix quick-play syntax remains supported.
- VI/EN panel labels and help text included.
- No database schema or economy migration.
